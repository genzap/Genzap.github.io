❓ FAQ
Is Genzap free? +
Yes, every device gets a few free images daily. If you need more, you can buy Credits.
Do free credits reset daily? +
Yes, your 10 free credits automatically renew every day at midnight — no login required. These are separate from purchased credits and don't carry forward to the next day.
How does Outfit Checker work? +
Outfit Checker is one of the AI Photo Packs (Value Plan, ₹99). Upload your photo and a photo of a clothing item, and the AI creates 4 looks of you wearing it for 12 credits. Looks that fail are refunded automatically. For best results, use a clear photo of one outfit.
How are credits used — free first or purchased? +
Your free daily credits are used first. Only after those run out will your purchased credits be deducted. This makes your paid credits last longer.
Do purchased credits ever expire? +
No, once purchased, credits never expire — use them whenever you like.
Where can I see my credits balance? +
After logging in, your total credits show in the top navbar on every page. A separate breakdown of free vs. purchased credits is on the "Your Credits" page (💎 icon).
How do I pay to buy credits? +
Right now Genzap only accepts manual UPI payments. Tapping "Buy Now" gives you a QR code and UPI ID — pay by scanning with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) or using the "Pay in UPI App" button.
When do credits arrive after payment? +
After paying, send a screenshot along with your Reference Code to genzap01@gmail.com. Credits are usually added within a few hours, and take at most 24 hours.
What's the Reference Code and why does it matter? +
Every payment attempt gets a unique code (like GZ-XXXXX). It helps us identify which account and which pack the payment is for — so credits get added quickly and to the right account. Always include this code when sending your screenshot.
Is this UPI ID Genzap's own? +
Genzap is currently a small, solo-run team, so payments go through a verified personal UPI account until a business payment gateway is set up. Every payment is manually verified before credits are added.
I paid but haven't received credits — what now? +
First check that you've emailed the screenshot and Reference Code. If it's been more than 24 hours, email genzap01@gmail.com again with the payment screenshot, date/time, and your account email.
When does the free daily limit reset? +
The limit resets every day at midnight, based on your device's local time.
How do credits work? +
1 credit = 1 text-to-image generation. 3 credits = 1 photo upload-edit (editing costs us more to run). Credits never expire.
Is my photo safe? +
Yes. Uploaded photos are only used to generate your image. We never publicly share anyone's personal photo without permission — Community sharing is only for text-generated images; face-edit images are never made public.
Payment failed but money was deducted? +
In such cases, the amount is automatically refunded by your bank within 5-7 days. If you didn't get credits, email us at genzap01@gmail.com with the payment screenshot — we'll add credits right away.
Are refunds available? +
Unused purchased credits can be refunded if requested within 7 days. Credits that have already been used are not refundable.
How do I view or delete my history? +
Go to the My History page — every image is shown there, and you can download it, reuse its prompt, or delete it.
What is the AI Photo Pack? +
With Photo Pack, you upload just 1 front-facing selfie and pick a theme you like (like Royal Wedding or Corporate Studio). AI creates 5 different studio photos in that style without changing your face — for 15 credits. This feature is available to Value Plan (₹99) and Pro Plan users.
What if a look fails in Photo Pack? +
That failed look's 3 credits are automatically refunded — you're only charged for looks that were successfully generated.
Does my name show when I share to Community? +
No, only the image is shown — no name or identity is displayed.
How do I delete my account? +
You can send a request via the "Delete Account" option in your Profile settings — it's processed within 48 hours.

ℹ️ About Genzap

Genzap started with a simple frustration: turning a good photo into something trend-worthy shouldn't require design skills, expensive software, or knowing the "right" prompt to type into an AI chatbot.

What We're Building

Genzap is a photo and trend studio, built specifically around how people in India actually want to use AI — quickly, visually, and without typing paragraphs of instructions. Browse a trending style, upload your photo, and get your result in seconds. No prompt-writing required.

Why It's Different

Most AI tools are built for everyone, everywhere — which means they're built for no one in particular. Genzap focuses on what's actually trending here: festival looks, wedding and pre-wedding styles, and the visual trends moving through Instagram and WhatsApp right now. We add new styles regularly, based on what's actually trending.

Where We're Based

Genzap is built and run out of Motihari, Bihar, India 🇮🇳 — a small team working to make good design tools accessible to everyone, not just people who already know how to use them.
